Why use StickItUP for web annotation?

Themes & Typography

Choose from 6 pastel backgrounds and 4 curated fonts to match your workflow.

Drag & Pin

Place notes anywhere. Resize them freely. Pin them in place so they never move.

Notes Directory

Search, preview, or delete any note from the extension dashboard.

Truly Private

Notes live entirely in your browser. No accounts, sync servers, or analytics.

Install in under a minute.

  1. Download the latest .zip from Releases.
  2. Extract the archive to any folder on your computer.
  3. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  4. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  5. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
